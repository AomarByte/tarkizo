# Sound credits

The ambient recordings in `sounds/` come from the open-source project
[Moodist](https://github.com/remvze/moodist). Its README states that its sounds are
licensed under the **Pixabay Content License** or **CC0**. Both allow free use, including
commercial use, without attribution; the Pixabay license does not allow selling or
redistributing the sounds on their own as standalone files.

For TARKIZO the files were loudness-normalised (−24 LUFS) and re-encoded to 80 kbps MP3.
